The strategy · trend-filtered basket vs buy & hold
Trend-filtered basket (hold trending coins, else cash) BTC buy & hold Equal-weight buy & hold
Growth of $1, log scale, net of 30 bps round-trip cost. The basket holds an equal slice of every coin currently trending and sits in cash on the rest, so it averages roughly two-thirds in cash. That de-risking is why it earns a higher return than BTC with about half the volatility and less than half the drawdown, and holds up across both halves of the history.
Time-of-day clock · 24/7 UTC structure
Average hourly return by UTC hour, pooled across coins. A durable pattern: the US-afternoon hours (around 21:00-22:00 UTC) are reliably positive, while 23:00 and 10:00 UTC are reliably negative. Too small to trade after spreads, but useful as execution timing: if buying, prefer a flat/cheap hour over an expensive one.
