WHAT I DO

SERVICES.

00

DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE AUDIT

The right starting point for most engagements. A focused review of your Meta, Google, TikTok, domain, tracking, and access setup. You receive an asset map, risk list, priority fixes, and a recommended recovery plan. No ongoing commitment required.

01

PLATFORM GOVERNANCE

Full audit and rebuild of Meta Business Suite, Google Ads, and TikTok environments. Asset mapping, access hierarchy documentation, and clean structure from scratch. Reduces platform risk, establishes clear ownership, and creates a stable foundation for paid media.

02

ASSET RECOVERY

Orphaned Business Managers, locked ad accounts, inaccessible pages. I identify the cleanest recovery path, prepare escalation materials, manage support cases directly, and document what should happen next if recovery is not possible. Most teams don't know how this process works. I do.

03

DOMAIN & BUSINESS VERIFICATION

Domain verification, Meta business verification, attestation processes. The compliance layer that unlocks full platform functionality and keeps accounts in good standing. Often skipped by agencies. Always matters.

04

TRACKING DIAGNOSTICS

Pixel installation, event mapping, conversion tracking. Find out what's misfiring and fix it. This includes the booking engine and vendor layer that most agencies never touch — the gap between your website pixel and your actual lower-funnel conversion events.

05

NGO & NONPROFIT COMPLIANCE

Special Ad Category navigation, Meta nonprofit program setup, Google Ad Grants application and management. The compliance structure that lets mission-driven organizations actually run ads — purpose-built for criminal justice, housing, and social justice organizations.

06

PAID MEDIA STRATEGY

Campaign architecture, audience frameworks, and full funnel strategy built on top of clean infrastructure. Prospecting, retargeting, lookalike audiences, and property-level positioning. Performance interpreted and translated into operational decisions — not handed over as a report nobody reads.

07

GOVERNANCE DOCUMENTATION

Asset ownership maps, access hierarchies, ad compliance guides, recovery protocols, and offboarding procedures. Safer vendor handoffs, cleaner transitions, and no dependency on one person's memory. Your org keeps the knowledge when the engagement ends.
