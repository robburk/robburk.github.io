WHAT I DO

SERVICES.

01

PLATFORM
GOVERNANCE

Most organizations have Meta, Google, and TikTok accounts that nobody fully controls — set up by agencies, former employees, or contractors, with no documentation and no clear ownership.

I audit the full environment, map every asset, restructure access, and rebuild from a clean foundation. You get a hierarchy that makes sense, with the right people holding the right permissions.

  • Meta Business Suite restructure
  • Google Ads account audit
  • TikTok Ads environment setup
  • Access hierarchy documentation
02

ASSET
RECOVERY

Pages, ad accounts, and Business Managers that belong to your organization but nobody can access. This happens constantly — and most teams don't know how to get them back.

I navigate Meta and Google support escalation paths to recover what you own. Locked accounts, orphaned pages, inaccessible Business Managers — the process is slow and frustrating without someone who knows how it works.

  • Meta Business Manager recovery
  • Facebook and Instagram page access
  • Ad account reinstatement
  • Appeals and support case management
03

TRACKING
DIAGNOSTICS

You're running paid media but your pixel is misfiring, your conversions aren't tracking, and you don't know what's actually working. This makes every optimization decision a guess.

I find what's broken, fix it, and set up proper event tracking that matches how your business actually converts — whether that's form fills, calls, purchases, or donation events.

  • Meta Pixel installation and QA
  • Conversion event mapping
  • Google Tag Manager setup
  • Nonprofit-specific event tracking
04

VERIFICATION
& COMPLIANCE

Domain verification, business verification, Special Ad Categories, nonprofit program eligibility — the compliance layer that unlocks full platform functionality and keeps accounts in good standing.

I handle the verification processes that agencies skip and internal teams don't understand. This is the work that prevents account restrictions before they happen.

  • Meta domain and business verification
  • Special Ad Category setup
  • Google Ad Grants application
  • NGO policy navigation

Every engagement includes full documentation — asset maps, access protocols, compliance guides, and offboarding procedures. The goal is that you never need to call me for the same problem twice.
