LUXURY HOSPITALITY  ·  TURKS AND CAICOS  ·  9-MONTH ENGAGEMENT

GRACE BAY RESORTS

CLIENT
Luxury hospitality portfolio, Turks and Caicos
DURATION
3-month contract expanded to 9 months
SCOPE
Meta governance, asset recovery, tracking diagnostics, paid media strategy, team training
PORTFOLIO
10+ properties
CORE ISSUE
Fragmented ownership, broken tracking, no single source of truth across any system
RESULT
Verified, restructured, documented, and campaign-ready digital infrastructure
SITUATION

Grace Bay Resorts operates 10+ luxury properties across Turks and Caicos — including Grace Bay Club, Rock House, West Bay Club, South Bank, Point Grace, The Point, Coco, Infiniti, and the Private Villa Collection. The original contract was three months of technical Meta support. It ran nine.

When the engagement started, Meta assets were spread across personal accounts, former staff, legacy agencies, and Business Managers nobody could access. Domains were unverified. Ad accounts were fragmented and tied to individuals rather than the company. Conversion tracking was incomplete or not firing at all across booking engines. There was no internal technical capacity to fix any of it and no documentation of what they owned.

GOVERNANCE AND ASSET RECOVERY

Full audit of the entire Meta environment across all properties. Every page, ad account, pixel, and Business Manager was mapped. A clean Business Portfolio was built from scratch and all properties were reconnected under proper company ownership. Business verification and domain verification were completed for the group.

Asset recovery was required across every property in the portfolio. Each involved some combination of page transfer, ownership correction, permission restructuring, or account relinking. Many were tied to disabled accounts or inaccessible legacy portfolios. Meta support cases were opened and managed directly. Escalation materials and attestation letters were drafted and submitted on the company's behalf.

One property required a different approach entirely. The Point's Facebook page was tied to a disabled admin account, a disabled legacy Instagram, and an inaccessible Business Manager. Every standard release and transfer path was attempted and failed. A merge request was submitted to Meta. After exhausting all options, the strategic call was made to build a clean replacement page inside the GBR Business Portfolio, link the active Instagram account, and retire the legacy asset. Clean governance was worth more than preserving a 300-follower page with no recoverable ownership chain.

Separately, the Infiniti Restaurant and Raw Bar Facebook page had been automatically age-gated by Meta due to alcohol-referenced post copy. The fix was fast once identified — audience restrictions set to 18+ at the page level. It was flagged as a standard audit item for all hospitality properties going forward.

TRACKING DIAGNOSTICS

Meta pixel setup was audited across the key properties. The issue was not the base website pixel — it was the booking engine event layer. Lower funnel events including InitiateCheckout, AddPaymentInfo, and Purchase were not firing, which meant paid media optimization was running blind on the most valuable conversion signals.

The booking engine vendors — SynXis, LHW, SLH, and Preferred — were contacted and mapped individually. LHW confirmed no Meta pixel scripts existed in their booking flow at all. Preferred confirmed they do not configure GTM tracking unless explicitly instructed. SLH confirmed scripts were implemented for Point Grace and South Bank. Vendor responsibilities were documented, GTM implementation guidance was issued across the portfolio, and attribution was corrected where technically possible.

PAID MEDIA STRATEGY

Once the infrastructure was clean, the engagement expanded into paid media strategy across the full portfolio. The team had been running boosted posts. The work moved them to structured Ads Manager campaigns with a proper full funnel — prospecting, retargeting, and lookalike audiences built on the corrected pixel data.

A feeder market targeting framework was built around airline access patterns, ZIP-level affluence indicators, and geographic prioritization. Each property was positioned differently — flagship, ultra luxury, boutique, family, and villa products require different messaging and audience logic. Campaign performance was interpreted and translated into operational decisions throughout, not handed over as a report.

TRAINING AND CAPABILITY BUILDING

The internal marketing team was trained across Meta campaign structure, audience building, retargeting logic, pixel setup, funnel strategy, and performance interpretation. Workshops covered campaign hierarchy, Ads Manager workflows, and how technical constraints affect marketing execution. The goal was that the team could operate independently — not remain dependent on outside support for basic decisions.

Throughout the engagement, technical translation was a constant part of the work — between the marketing team, booking engine vendors, and Meta support. Stakeholder communications, status updates, and transition documentation were prepared and delivered at each stage.

NONPROFIT / NGO  ·  NEW YORK CITY

GOOD CALL NYC

SITUATION

Good Call is a New York City-based criminal justice nonprofit that provides free legal support to people who have been arrested. They had been locked out of Meta's advertising platform for years — flagged as a political or NGO organization, their page banned, with no internal team capable of navigating the appeals process. No Business Manager, no ad account, no clear path forward.

WHAT I DID

Started from scratch. A new clean Meta Business Portfolio was created and business verification was completed. The environment was structured correctly for a nonprofit operating in social issues categories — which requires specific compliance setup that most agencies are not familiar with. Domain verification was completed and documented for the web team to implement.

Nonprofits operating in criminal justice, housing, and social justice categories fall under Meta's Special Ad Category restrictions — which affect audience targeting, ad delivery, and compliance requirements. Building the environment without understanding those restrictions means ads get rejected or the account gets flagged again. The setup was structured to work within those constraints from the start.

A Google Ad Grants opportunity was identified. Good Call qualifies as a 501(c)(3) for Google's nonprofit program, which provides up to $10,000 per month in free search advertising. The application pathway was scoped and the process initiated through Google's current nonprofit verification partner.

OUTCOME
Meta Business Portfolio rebuilt from zero Business verified for social issues ad categories Domain verification completed New ad account created, clean environment Google Ad Grants path scoped ($10k/mo) Governance documentation delivered
