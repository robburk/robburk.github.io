LUXURY HOSPITALITY  ·  TURKS AND CAICOS

GRACE BAY RESORTS

SITUATION

Grace Bay Resorts is one of the leading luxury hospitality groups in Turks and Caicos, operating 10+ properties including some of the most recognized resorts in the Caribbean. When the engagement began, their digital infrastructure told a different story — Meta assets spread across personal accounts, Business Managers nobody could access, unverified domains, and ad accounts that hadn't been properly structured in years. There was no internal technical capacity to fix it and no clear picture of what they even owned.

WHAT I DID

Full audit of the entire Meta environment across all properties. Every page, ad account, pixel, and Business Manager was mapped and documented. From there, the rebuild started — a clean Business Portfolio created, all properties reconnected under proper ownership, business and domain verification completed, and ad accounts restructured to be compliant and functional.

One of the property restaurant pages had been automatically age-gated by Meta due to alcohol-referenced post copy — a common issue that most teams never catch. The fix is fast but requires knowing it's there. That was resolved and flagged for all future content.

Pixel and conversion tracking was audited and corrected across the key properties. Meta support cases were opened and managed directly. Staff were trained on the new structure. A full governance documentation package was delivered — asset maps, access hierarchy, ad compliance guidelines, and offboarding protocols.

OUTCOME
Meta Business Portfolio verified and fully restructured 10+ property pages recovered and reconnected Business and domain verification completed Pixel and conversion tracking corrected Age-gate issue identified and resolved Staff trained on governance structure Full documentation package delivered Meta support cases managed end-to-end
NONPROFIT / NGO  ·  NEW YORK CITY

GOOD CALL NYC

SITUATION

Good Call is a New York City-based criminal justice nonprofit that provides free legal support to people who have been arrested. They had been effectively locked out of Meta's advertising platform for years — flagged as a political or NGO organization, their page banned, with no internal team capable of navigating the appeals process. No Business Manager, no ad account, no clear path forward.

WHAT I DID

Started from scratch. A new, clean Meta Business Portfolio was created and business verification was completed. The environment was structured correctly for a nonprofit operating in social issues categories — which requires specific compliance setup that most agencies don't know exists. The domain verification process was completed and documented for the web team to implement.

A Google Ad Grants opportunity was identified — Good Call qualifies as a 501(c)(3) for Google's nonprofit program, which provides up to $10,000 per month in free search advertising. The application pathway was scoped and the process initiated through Google's current nonprofit verification partner.

OUTCOME
Meta Business Portfolio rebuilt from zero Business verified for social issues ad categories Domain verification completed New ad account created, clean environment Google Ad Grants path scoped ($10k/mo) Governance documentation delivered
