WHO I AM

ROBIN

BACKGROUND

I'm a digital governance consultant based in New York. I work with multi-brand companies, luxury hospitality groups, and nonprofits to fix the digital infrastructure that paid media depends on.

My background is in platform operations — navigating Meta, Google, and TikTok environments at a level that most agencies and internal teams don't operate at. I specialize in the unglamorous work that unlocks everything else: asset recovery, governance structure, compliance, and tracking.

I come in from the outside, which means I see things internal teams miss. I don't have a stake in the existing setup. I just fix it.

APPROACH

Every engagement starts with a full audit. I map what exists, identify what's broken or at risk, and build a clear picture of the infrastructure before recommending anything.

The rebuild is documented. The client ends up with a governance structure they actually understand and can maintain. The goal is never to create dependency — it's to leave things better than I found them.
