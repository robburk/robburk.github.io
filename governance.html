THE PROBLEM

INFRASTRUCTURE NOBODY OWNS.

Every company that runs paid media has a back-end — Business Managers, ad accounts, pixels, pages, domains. Over time, this infrastructure breaks down. Employees leave. Access gets lost. Assets end up tied to personal accounts. Nobody knows what they have or who owns it.

This isn't a marketing problem. It's a governance problem. And it's almost always invisible until something stops working.

THE SOLUTION

OUTSIDE EYES. CLEAN SETUP.

I come in as an outside professional, audit the full digital environment, identify what's broken or at risk, and rebuild it correctly. Every asset gets documented. Every access level gets mapped. The org knows exactly what it owns and who controls it.

The result is infrastructure that supports paid media instead of blocking it.

WHO NEEDS THIS

MORE COMMON THAN YOU THINK.

Multi-brand companies. Hospitality groups. NGOs and nonprofits. Any organization that has been running paid media for a few years and has never done a proper infrastructure audit.

If your agency has ever said "we can't run ads because of an account issue" — this is why.
